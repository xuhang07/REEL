# Image purpose: first frame

The image will be the complete frame zero of the video shot.

- Depict the exact initial instant from which the requested video action can
  proceed. Include every visible subject's starting pose, orientation, gaze,
  position, held object, and spatial relationship, plus the intended camera,
  composition, environment, and lighting.
- Do not depict the requested action as already completed. Do not montage
  multiple times or views into one image.
- Favor a clean, coherent full-frame composition that can pass condition
  readiness and be consumed as `generate_shot.condition` with relation
  `frame0_match`.
- Do not claim motion, dialogue, audio, or later-shot outcomes in the still
  prompt.
